This Refund Policy explains when and how SisterMart issues refunds, together with the Return Policy, Shipping Policy and User Agreement. A refund is not automatic: it must meet this Policy and the Return Policy and pass our review/inspection. To protect lawful merchant interests and prevent abuse, we may verify the transaction, adjust the refund breakdown, pause or stage processing, and refuse or partially refund where reasonable (except where mandatory law requires otherwise).

1. When we may refund

  • After a return under the Return Policy and a passed inbound inspection
  • After successful payment, where we fail to ship for a prolonged period due to our reasons and you choose to cancel a cancellable order we confirm
  • Verified duplicate charges, pricing system errors, clearly overcharged shipping, or other amount errors attributable to us
  • Wrong/missing items where both sides agree to refund instead of reship/exchange
  • Other cases we agree to refund in writing

2. No refund or partial refund only

  • Requests that fail the Return Policy or inspection
  • Used promotional benefits, non-reversible third-party fees, or depreciation/missing-parts portions
  • Extra logistics costs from wrong address, refusal or unclaimed parcels (may be deducted)
  • Gifts, packing consumables or costs already incurred and non-returnable (case by case)
  • Suspected fraud, item swapping, chargeback abuse or after-sales abuse

3. Refund composition

  1. Item price: generally the amount actually paid for that item (after allocating discounts).
  2. Order promos / thresholds / coupons: if remaining items no longer meet a campaign threshold after refund, we may claw back the related discount (deduct from refund or require a top-up).
  3. Points: points used at checkout may be restored by rule; points earned from the order may be clawed back. Points-mall orders may have stricter refund limits.
  4. Shipping:
    • Quality issues / wrong items: original outbound shipping and approved return shipping may be refunded or reimbursed within reason
    • Non-quality returns: original outbound shipping is usually non-refundable; return/reship shipping is yours
    • Partial returns that break free-shipping eligibility: we may deduct the shipping then due
  5. Handling / inspection / restocking: for change-of-mind or imperfect-condition cases still handled by agreement, we may deduct reasonable fees before refunding the balance.

4. Refund method

  • Refunds generally go to the original payment method.
  • If that channel cannot receive funds or has expired, we may notify you and use another reasonable method (store credit, coupon goodwill, or an agreed transfer).
  • We do not guarantee cash or instant non-original-path refunds; provider rules apply.

5. Timing

  • Start date: from the day we complete inspection (if any) and confirm “refund approved” in our system—not from your request or ship-back date.
  • Our processing: after confirmation we usually submit the refund instruction within 3–15 business days (reconciliation, risk checks, holidays or provider review may extend this).
  • Arrival: banks/payment providers often need additional business days; we cannot control their speed.
  • Multiple after-sales on one order, open disputes or missing evidence may pause the refund until clarified.

6. Cancellation vs shipped orders

  • Not yet shipped: you may request cancellation; success depends on warehouse progress. Once picking/handover started, cancellation may fail and the Return Policy applies.
  • Already shipped: “changed my mind” alone does not entitle an instant full online refund; goods must return and pass inspection under the Return Policy.
  • If you open a payment dispute/chargeback, we may pause parallel handling and rely on the dispute record, while retaining the right to submit logistics and communication evidence.

7. Partial refunds and exchange balances

Multi-item orders may be refunded only for eligible lines. Exchange price differences are settled (refund or top-up); we may hold the replacement until any top-up is paid. Shipping and promo recalculation follow Section 3.

8. Records

After refund, orders, points and benefits follow system records. For invoice/receipt adjustments, contact support after the refund completes; we will help within reason.

9. Discretion and anti-abuse

Where law allows, SisterMart may make a reasonable commercial decision on disputed cases (partial refund, delay, goodwill instead of cash, or refusal). For abnormal return frequency, chargebacks or false statements, we may limit purchase/after-sales access and recover refunded sums and related losses.
