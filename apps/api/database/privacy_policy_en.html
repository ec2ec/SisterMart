Depending on how you use the Service, we may collect the following (not all items are always collected):

  • Email address and mobile number (including country/area code, e.g. +852)

3.3 Order, delivery and payment-related data

  • Recipient name, phone number and delivery address fields you provide

3.4 Membership operations and engagement data

  • Points balance, points ledger and points-mall redemption records
  • Daily check-in and streak reward records
  • Product favourites and browsing footprints
  • Coupon claim/use records
  • Distribution/referral relationships (e.g. referral codes and commission settlement data), only if you join a distribution programme

  • Newsletter subscription status and email

3.5 Customer support and communications

  • Messages, attachments and contact preferences you provide via WhatsApp, email or in-app channels
  • Operational notes/tickets created to handle your enquiry

3.6 Technical, device and local storage data

  • Browser type, OS, approximate device/screen info, language and time zone
  • IP address, request timestamps and error logs (for security, debugging and abuse prevention)
  • Cookies and localStorage/sessionStorage (see Section 8), such as sign-in state, cart, language, temporary site-access flags and UI tip states

3.7 Other data you voluntarily provide

For example information you submit in forms, campaigns or support chats. Please do not send sensitive data unrelated to the Service (e.g. full identity document numbers) unless we expressly request it for a stated purpose.

  • Directly from you: registration/sign-in, addresses, checkout, reviews, after-sales, subscriptions, support chats, etc.

5. Purposes of collection and use

In line with PDPO purpose and use-limitation principles, we use personal data only for:

  1. Account management: creating, verifying, maintaining and securing your membership; sign-in/out, password reset and OTP verification
  2. Order fulfilment: cart, checkout, stock checks, payment confirmation, records needed for reconciliation, delivery and receipt follow-up
  3. Customer service and after-sales: answering queries, returns/after-sales handling, review moderation and dispute coordination
  4. Membership benefits and operations: points issuance/redemption, check-in rewards, coupons, favourites/footprints, distribution commission settlement (where applicable)
  5. Necessary notices: transactional/service messages about account security, order status, payment results, shipment and after-sales progress (generally not dependent on marketing consent)
  6. Marketing with consent: offers, new arrivals or campaigns where you subscribed to a newsletter or otherwise gave express consent; you may unsubscribe at any time
  7. Security and compliance: preventing fraud, abuse and unauthorised access; detecting anomalous sign-ins; complying with law, regulators or law enforcement; keeping the Service stable
  8. Service improvement: analysing usage in aggregated or de-identified form where practicable to improve experience and flows (we do not sell personal data for this)

If we intend to use data for a new purpose not directly related to the original purpose, we will obtain your prescribed consent as required by the PDPO, or rely on another lawful basis permitted by law.

6. Direct marketing

If we intend to use your name, email, phone number or similar data for direct marketing (e.g. promotions), we will, under PDPO s.35 and related rules:

  • Inform you of the classes of data and marketing subjects, and obtain your consent (prescribed consent) before sending; and/or
  • Provide an easy opt-out (unsubscribe link, account settings or customer service)

Transactional notices (orders, payments, logistics, security verification, etc.) are not direct marketing. Even if you opt out of promotions, we may still send those necessary messages.

We use cookies and browser local storage to:

  • Maintain sign-in state and session security

  • Support temporary site-access / anti-abuse or operational tip states

You can clear or restrict cookies/local storage in your browser; some features (persistent sign-in, cart) may then stop working. We do not by default deploy third-party advertising tracking networks unrelated to core shopping; if we introduce non-essential tracking later, we will explain and offer choices where applicable.

For operations, personal data may be transferred to or stored on servers outside Hong Kong, or processed by overseas processors (e.g. cloud hosting, email/messaging). We take reasonable steps so that recipients provide protection comparable to PDPO requirements, and transfer only what is needed to provide the Service.

10. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes collected, then delete, destroy or anonymise it in a reasonable manner. Typical periods (which may be extended for legal holds or unresolved disputes):

  • Account data: while the account remains active, plus a reasonable period after closure/deletion for residual obligations and fraud prevention
  • Order, payment and invoice-related records: generally for commercial, accounting and tax retention needs (commonly at least 7 years after the transaction; actual period follows applicable law and internal policy)
  • OTP and security logs: a shorter period limited to verification, abuse prevention and security audit
  • Support and after-sales records: a reasonable period after the dispute/after-sales window ends
  • Marketing subscription lists: until you unsubscribe or we end the programme
  • Reviews and user-generated content: while needed for display and operations; we will handle deletion requests reasonably where law does not require retention

11. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures, including HTTPS, password hashing, access controls, admin authentication, input validation, backups and anomaly monitoring. Internet transmission is not perfectly secure; please protect your password and device and never share OTPs. Contact us immediately and reset your password if you suspect account misuse.
