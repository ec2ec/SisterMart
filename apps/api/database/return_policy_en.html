This Return Policy applies to SisterMart (sistermart.vip) Hong Kong orders and should be read with the Refund Policy, Shipping Policy and User Agreement. By requesting a return/exchange you agree to this Policy. To keep trade fair and the shop sustainable, we may review requests, ask for evidence, refuse ineligible claims, and handle cases with reasonable discretion.

1. Time limits

  • After-sales requests must generally be submitted within 15 days of delivery receipt (or a shorter period stated at checkout/on the product page) via the order after-sales flow or customer service. Late requests without a reasonable excuse may be refused.
  • Quality issues / major misdescription must also be raised within that window, with clear photos or video.
  • Campaign, clearance, pre-order or product-page rules that set a shorter or non-returnable term prevail for those items.

2. Eligible situations (all applicable conditions must be met)

  1. Quality defect or serious misdescription: a material defect affecting normal use, or a clear mismatch with key product-page facts (excluding reasonable colour/feel/lighting variance or subjective preference).
  2. Transit damage: obvious shipping damage to outer box or item—please document with photos ASAP after receipt (ideally within 48 hours) and contact us.
  3. Wrong / missing item: after we verify the order, we may reship, exchange or follow the Refund Policy.
  4. Other cases we agree to in writing: non-defect returns (e.g. change of mind) only if the item meets Section 4 and we pre-approve; we may charge a reasonable handling/restocking cost or require you to pay shipping.

3. Non-returnable / refusals

We may refuse return/exchange or offer only limited help (unless mandatory law requires otherwise) where:

  • the item was worn, tried for prolonged use, stained, scratched, bent, altered, cut or washed
  • original packaging, tags, seals, accessories, certificates or packing list are missing and resale is affected
  • hygiene-related items (e.g. opened/tried ear studs, intimate jewellery care consumables, opened beauty consumables)
  • custom, engraved, resized/re-chained, made-to-order, or clearly marked non-returnable items
  • clearance, mystery bags, free gifts, or points-mall redemptions (unless otherwise stated)
  • issues caused by preference, wrong size estimate, reasonable colour variance, or failure to follow care instructions
  • the request is late, lacks valid order/receipt info, or you refuse inspection/evidence
  • fraud, serial abusive returns, item swapping or clear after-sales abuse is found

4. Condition of returned goods

Except confirmed defects where original condition cannot be restored, returns must:

  • be new/near-new and resalable
  • include original/outbound packaging, accessories and gifts
  • be unworn (jewellery must be unscratched, unaltered, not heavily exposed to chemicals/perfume)

We may open, inspect and photograph returns. If condition, missing parts or description do not match, we may refuse return/refund, ask you to send missing parts, switch to exchange, or proceed only after deducting depreciation/missing parts/shipping (as notified in writing).

5. Process

  1. Submit after-sales on the order, or email service@sistermart.vip / WhatsApp +852 5598 3063 with order number, items, description and evidence.
  2. Wait for approval. Then ship to the address we provide. Unauthorised returns may be refused; related costs are yours.
  3. Pack carefully and keep the shipping proof. Risk of loss/damage in transit is generally yours unless we confirmed a quality issue and agreed to cover return shipping.
  4. After inbound inspection passes, we arrange exchange or start the Refund Policy flow. Inspection and internal handling usually take several business days.

6. Shipping and costs

  • Non-quality returns (including approved change-of-mind): return shipping, reship shipping and any handling/inspection/restocking costs are generally yours; original outbound shipping refunds follow the Refund Policy.
  • Confirmed quality issues / wrong items: we cover reasonable return shipping via our instructed method (excess or unnecessary express fees may be excluded).
  • Returns caused by wrong address, refusal or long unclaimed parcels may require you to pay reship fees.

7. Exchanges

Exchanges depend on stock and are not guaranteed. If the same item is unavailable, we may offer alternatives (wait, swap for another in-stock item with balance settlement, or refund per the Refund Policy). Exchanged goods follow the same after-sales rules as new items once shipped.

8. Evidence and records

We may request unboxing video, close-ups, packaging shots and courier labels. Failure to provide evidence within a reasonable time may be treated as withdrawal. Support chats, system logs and inspection results may be used to decide the case.

9. Discretion and final handling

For borderline cases, SisterMart may make a reasonable discretionary decision after verification (partial refund, exchange only, spare parts, coupon goodwill, or refusal), and will explain the main reasons. For bad-faith disputes or false statements, we may limit after-sales, cancel benefits or seek remedies under law.

10. Contact and updates

Contact service@sistermart.vip or WhatsApp +852 5598 3063. We may update this Policy; the latest version on this page applies. Requests already accepted before an update are generally handled under the rules at submission time, unless law requires otherwise.
